## Dataset Overview

### DataCo Smart Supply Chain Dataset

The **DataCo Smart Supply Chain Dataset** is a publicly available dataset from Kaggle that provides detailed information about DataCo Global's supply chain operations. It contains data related to sales transactions, customers, products, orders, shipping, delivery performance, and financial metrics.

The original dataset contains approximately **180,519 records and 53 columns**, covering different aspects of the order-to-delivery process. It includes information about product categories, customer segments, order dates, shipping methods, sales revenue, order profitability, and delivery status.

### Key Data Areas

- **Sales and Financial Performance:** Sales revenue, order totals, discounts, and profit-related metrics.
- **Customer Analysis:** Customer information, customer segments, and geographical locations.
- **Product Analysis:** Product names, categories, prices, and quantities ordered.
- **Order Analysis:** Order IDs, order dates, order status, and transaction types.
- **Shipping and Delivery:** Shipping modes, actual and scheduled shipping days, delivery status, and late delivery risk.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Markets, countries, cities, and regions associated with orders and customers.

### Dataset Purpose

This dataset is suitable for analyzing business performance and identifying opportunities to improve sales, profitability, customer understanding, and supply chain efficiency.

In this project, the dataset is used to build an integrated sales analytics solution by combining **Python, SQL Server, and Power BI**. Python supports data exploration and cleaning, SQL Server is used to store and query the data, and Power BI transforms the processed data into interactive dashboards and visual reports.

### Main Analytical Objectives

- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), including total sales, total profit, total orders, and total customers.
- Analyze sales and profitability across markets, regions, and product categories.
- Identify top-performing products and customer segments.
- Compare business performance across different years and time periods.
- Evaluate shipping performance and identify potential delivery issues.
- Generate actionable insights to support data-driven business decisions.
